Adobe Ridge Systems LLC

Industrial Control Electronics • Designed for Critical Infrastructure

Lender & Funding Overview

Adobe Ridge Systems LLC is a U.S.-based engineering company developing industrial-grade control electronics for critical infrastructure applications. The company’s initial deployment focus is wastewater and water treatment facilities—an infrastructure segment characterized by long equipment lifecycles, conservative purchasing behavior, and sustained demand driven by regulatory compliance and public necessity.

The company is currently in Engineering Validation (EVT) and early Design Validation (DVT) for its first control platform and is seeking funding to complete validation, pilot deployments, and initial small-batch manufacturing.

The Problem

  • Critical infrastructure operators—particularly municipal wastewater and water utilities—rely on aging control systems.
  • Systems can be difficult to service or replace, vulnerable to electrical noise and surges, and dependent on long lead times or discontinued components.
  • Many modern replacements prioritize feature density and cost reduction over long-term reliability and field maintainability.

Our Solution

Adobe Ridge Systems develops hardened industrial control PCBAs designed specifically for long service life, electrical robustness, and field serviceability.

  • MCU-based architecture using widely supported components
  • Isolated industrial communications (e.g., RS-485 / Modbus)
  • 4–20 mA analog input and control interfaces
  • Conservative power design with surge and transient protection
  • Clear test points and documentation for troubleshooting

The underlying control platform is intentionally designed to support a broader range of infrastructure and industrial automation applications over time.

Platform Expansion Potential

  • Infrastructure-focused OEM control subsystems

# Adobe Ridge Systems LLC ## Lender & Funding Overview **Industrial Control Electronics for Critical Infrastructure** --- ## Executive Summary Adobe Ridge Systems LLC is a U.S.-based engineering company developing industrial-grade control electronics for critical infrastructure applications. The company’s initial deployment focus is wastewater and water treatment facilities—an infrastructure segment characterized by long equipment lifecycles, conservative purchasing behavior, and sustained demand driven by regulatory compliance and public necessity. The company is currently in Engineering Validation (EVT) and early Design Validation (DVT) for its first control platform and is seeking funding to complete validation, pilot deployments, and initial small-batch manufacturing. --- ## The Problem Critical infrastructure operators—particularly municipal wastewater and water utilities—rely on aging control systems that are: * Difficult to service or replace * Vulnerable to electrical noise, surges, and environmental stress * Dependent on long lead times or discontinued components Modern replacements often prioritize feature density and cost reduction over long-term reliability and field maintainability, creating operational risk for utilities that require predictable, 24/7 operation. --- ## Our Solution Adobe Ridge Systems develops **hardened industrial control PCBAs** designed specifically for long service life, electrical robustness, and field serviceability. Key characteristics of the control platform include: * MCU-based architecture using widely supported components * Isolated industrial communications (e.g., RS-485 / Modbus) * 4–20 mA analog input and control interfaces * Conservative power design with surge and transient protection * Clear test points and documentation for troubleshooting The platform is intentionally designed to be adaptable across related infrastructure control use cases without redesigning core architecture. --- ## Initial Market Focus **Primary Deployment Vertical** * Wastewater treatment plants * Water treatment facilities These markets were selected due to: * Non-discretionary demand * Long asset lifecycles * Preference for reliable, supportable hardware over frequent upgrades * Ongoing regulatory and infrastructure investment --- ## Platform Expansion Potential Rather than pursuing single-purpose products, Adobe Ridge Systems follows a **platform-first engineering approach**. The same hardened control architecture can be adapted to adjacent infrastructure applications with similar electrical, environmental, and operational requirements. Potential adjacent applications include: * Pump and lift station control * Remote monitoring and telemetry systems * Industrial process supervision * Infrastructure-focused OEM control subsystems Expansion into these areas leverages the same core design, reducing development risk and capital requirements. --- ## Competitive Positioning Adobe Ridge Systems differentiates itself by focusing on: * Reliability and uptime over feature quantity * Serviceability and documentation * Conservative component selection and derating * Long-term support expectations aligned with infrastructure operators The company does not compete on consumer electronics cost structures, but on total cost of ownership and operational risk reduction. --- ## Current Phase & Milestones **Current Status** * Engineering Validation (EVT) * Early Design Validation (DVT) **Near-Term Milestones (Post-Funding)** * Completion of DVT * Pilot deployments with selected operators or integrators * Small-batch manufacturing * Field feedback incorporation n--- ## Funding Use (High-Level) Funding is intended to support: * Final hardware validation and testing * Compliance and pre-certification activities (as applicable) * Initial manufacturing runs * Documentation and deployment readiness Detailed financials, budgets, and timelines are available upon request. --- ## Company Profile **Legal Entity:** Adobe Ridge Systems LLC **Location:** United States **Focus:** Industrial control electronics for critical infrastructure **Stage:** Pre-revenue / Validation stage --- ## Contact **Business & Funding Inquiries** Email: [info@adoberidgesystems.com](mailto:info@adoberidgesystems.com) **Engineering & Technical Discussions** Email: [engineering@adoberidgesystems.com](mailto:engineering@adoberidgesystems.com) --- © Adobe Ridge Systems LLC.

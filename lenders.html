Executive Summary

Adobe Ridge Systems LLC is a U.S.-based engineering company developing industrial-grade control electronics for critical infrastructure applications. Our first deployment focus is wastewater treatment (and water treatment) facilities—an infrastructure segment with long equipment lifecycles, conservative procurement, and sustained demand driven by regulatory compliance and public necessity.

The company is in Engineering Validation (EVT) and early Design Validation (DVT) for its first control platform and is seeking funding to complete validation, pilot deployments, and initial small-batch manufacturing.

Core thesis: reliability-first electronics reduce operational risk and total cost of ownership for critical infrastructure operators.

The Problem

  • Aging control systems remain widely deployed across wastewater and water infrastructure.
  • Systems can be difficult to service, vulnerable to electrical noise/surges, or dependent on discontinued components.
  • Modern replacements often prioritize feature density and cost reduction over long-term maintainability.
  • Utilities require predictable 24/7 operation; downtime has outsized consequences.

Solution Overview

Adobe Ridge Systems develops hardened industrial control PCBAs designed for long service life, electrical robustness, and predictable field service.

Engineering characteristics
  • MCU-based architecture using widely supported components
  • Isolated industrial communications (RS-485 / Modbus)
  • 4–20 mA analog input and control interfaces
  • Conservative power design with surge/transient protection
Deployment readiness
  • Clear test points and documentation for troubleshooting
  • Design for noisy, long-cable industrial environments
  • Lifecycle-aware BOM philosophy
  • Field maintainability as a first-class requirement
Platform intent: a common hardened architecture adaptable to adjacent infrastructure control use cases without redesigning the core platform.
